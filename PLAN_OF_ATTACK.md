# Plan of attack

The operating decision is settled: caregiver-first self-directed research, engine-owned auditable rankings, and a licensed, appointed agent of record at purchase. The next decisions concern scope and evidence, not replacing this boundary. This is a dependency plan, not a calendar estimate.

Retirees themselves are direct users of the AI from the outset. They research their own coverage, save and resume, inspect comparisons and request licensed purchase review. Caregivers are an additional permissioned journey and the first acquisition priority.

## Workstreams alongside engineering

- **Insurance/operations:** review research/ranking as well as purchase by actual state/product/channel; establish entity/personnel authority, appointments, carrier test access, required materials and accountable complaint/service owners. Configure staffed hours, seasonal first-response targets and real scheduling from measured capacity. An auto-acknowledgment is not an agent response.
- **Quality/privacy:** define severe-error stop conditions, independent field/rule/cost review, consumer comprehension, permissions and retention. Test sensitive information stays out of generic analytics/partner trackers and that access revocation works. Passing structural tests is not insurance suitability evidence.
- **Distribution:** useful caregiver content/search first; immediate research entry, save/return and permissioned sharing. Financial-advisor software and publishers follow demonstrated direct demand, actual partner agreements and unchanged ranking across partner contexts. Health systems require actual published outcome evidence and separate role/data agreements.
- **Competitive learning:** maintain a dated incumbent-release watchlist and revisit the 12–24 month response hypothesis. Build caregiver brand recognition, working integrations, self-serve conversion knowledge and actual quality/retention evidence; no assumed permanent feature or labor advantage.

## Primary metric and gates

For a declared acquisition/handoff cohort and retention window:

**Human minutes per effective, retained policy = all attributable active human work minutes ÷ policies effective and still retained at that window.**

Include purchase review, re-verification, failed/abandoned cases, follow-up, corrections, service and exception work. Report queue waiting separately from active work. Declare shared-labor allocation and distinguish component policies from customers/bundles. A cohort with zero retained policies has no retained-policy outcome, not zero labor cost. Choose the retention window and baseline from actual operating/study decisions; no target reduction is claimed yet.

Keep suitability, comprehension, complaints, retention and wait/abandonment guardrails alongside efficiency. Measure contribution after acquisition, data, model, infrastructure, legal/compliance, licensed labor, support and reversals. Neither a lower model bill nor a shorter checklist proves a viable service.

Every package review should show real input/output, exact acceptance evidence, remaining unknowns, named dependencies and the smallest next change. Read-only experiments can advance with explicit uncertainty. Production purchase stays unavailable until the licensed-agent workflow and real operating gates are satisfied. Carrier-link self-enrollment remains disabled unless separately approved for an exact carrier/state/product/channel; queue overload does not change that boundary.
