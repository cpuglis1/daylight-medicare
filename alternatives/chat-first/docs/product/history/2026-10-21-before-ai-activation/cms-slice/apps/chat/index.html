About your answers

Confirmed guide answers can be saved on the server for this browser after you explicitly choose saving. No account is created. The protected guest cookie lets this browser resume; anyone sharing it may reopen the guide. The local prototype retention period is shown in Your information. Closing a tab does not delete a saved guide.

Original submitted text, unsent drafts and chat history are not stored. Optional names, dates, medicines, pharmacies, providers and preparation notes stay in the tab unless you separately choose to save them. Review and delete the saved guide in Your information.

AI interpretation is optional. Before sending, the app explains what is transferred to OpenAI. Sensitive lists and dates are not automatically attached. This service does not log submitted chat. Production vendor, retention and deployment review remain separate.

Plan identities shown as samples are an unvalidated subset. Personal recommendations, prices, provider or medicine fit, eligibility and enrollment are not provided. Use Medicare.gov or SHINE for checks.
