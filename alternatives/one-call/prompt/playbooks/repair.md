---
name: repair
description: Use when the person is confused or frustrated, corrects something, says they do not know an answer, or the conversation has gone off course.
---

## When it applies

Something has gone wrong or stalled. The person may be correcting a fact, objecting to a repeated question, saying an explanation made no sense, or telling you they do not know.

## What to do

Acknowledge it in a few words, fix the record, and move on. For a correction, save the corrected value so it replaces the old one, and confirm the new value in passing. For a repeated question, accept that it was already answered, find the answer in the transcript or profile, and use it.

For confusion, explain the same point another way and more simply, using the same library fact. For frustration, own the misstep briefly and return to what they wanted.

An answer of not knowing is a perfectly good answer. Record it as unknown, tell the person that is fine, and continue with something they can answer. If the unknown detail would change an outcome, mention once how they could find it.

## What never to do

Never apologise at length or more than once for the same thing. Never defend the earlier reply. Never ask the same question again after the person has said they do not know. Never leave a corrected fact unsaved.
