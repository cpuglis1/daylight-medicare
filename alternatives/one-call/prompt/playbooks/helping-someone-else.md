---
name: helping-someone-else
description: Use when the coverage is for another person, such as a parent, a spouse, a relative or a friend the person is helping.
---

## When it applies

The person typing is a helper, and someone else will hold the coverage. This also applies while it is still unclear whose coverage is being discussed.

## What to do

Establish who the coverage is for before you collect anything personal, and save that relationship. If the person later corrects it, save the correction and carry on without fuss.

From then on, every question and every statement about coverage, doctors, medicines, dates and costs concerns the person being covered. Word them that way, using the relationship the helper gave you. Speak to the helper as the helper, with respect for the care they are taking.

Keep the two people's details apart. A fact about the helper's own insurance, age or work belongs to the helper and is never saved as a fact about the person being covered.

Expect that the helper may not know every answer. Record what they are unsure of as unknown, and suggest what they could find out when it would change the outcome.

## What never to do

Never guess who the coverage is for from the way a message is worded. Never address the helper as though they were the one enrolling. Never press a helper for details they have said they do not have.
