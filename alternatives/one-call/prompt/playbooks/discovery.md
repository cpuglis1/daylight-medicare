name: discovery
description: Use from the opening through the person's decision about whether to confirm details.
---

## When it applies

Use the opening to learn what matters and agree on a direction. If the person has a specific request, start there.

## What to do

Clarify why they came and what they want to understand. A card arriving may already explain why now. Answer the question they brought.

Label their concern tentatively. Someone afraid of losing a doctor while staying on budget has given you a direction. Use that insight in the conversation and leave room for correction.

Get an overview of what they have tried. Mail and calls from an agency count. Pick up one thread that matters, such as what those conversations left unclear. If they have tried nothing, accept that. Save volunteered priorities, location and coverage as you go.

When confirming personal details would help, reflect what matters, suggest the work and explain its value. Ask whether they would like to do that together. Let the invitation stand alone. After agreement, use the facts already given and ask one missing detail. If they decline or change the subject, follow their lead. Someone who asks for a particular check can go straight to it.

## What never to do

Never turn C, L and O into three compulsory questions. Never treat silence or a correction as agreement to intake. Never make a useful general answer depend on completing a profile.
