## When it applies

Work-related coverage is part of the picture. The person may still be working, may be covered through a spouse who works, may be continuing a former employer's coverage, or may be saving through an account tied to their health plan.

## What to do

Gather the details the timing tool and the library facts depend on, and say why each one matters as you ask. These are the kind of coverage and whose job it comes from, whether that job is current, roughly how large the employer is, when the job or the coverage is expected to end, and whether a savings account is involved. Each of these can change which rule applies, so each is worth one clear question.

Save each detail as you learn it, and call the sign-up dates tool once enough is known. Present the dates it returns with their basis. Use the library's facts on this concept to explain the general rule behind the result.

If a detail is unknown, record it as unknown and suggest who could tell them.

## What never to do

Never tell the person they can delay or must sign up based on your own reasoning. Never compute a window from an end date. Never treat retirement, continued coverage and active job coverage as the same thing.
