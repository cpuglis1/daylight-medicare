Self-hosted webfonts for Daylight Medicare
===========================================

Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(https://scripts.sil.org/OFL), which permits bundling and self-hosting.

1. Atkinson Hyperlegible  — atkinson-400.woff2, atkinson-700.woff2
   © Braille Institute of America, Inc.
   Source: https://www.brailleinstitute.org/freefont/
   Designed for maximum legibility for low-vision readers (distinct
   l/I/1 and O/0 letterforms — important for dates, ZIPs and dollar amounts).

2. Fraunces (variable: opsz, wght) — fraunces.woff2
   © The Undercase Type Company
   Source: https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces
   Used for display headings only.

The woff2 files here are the Latin subsets retrieved from Google Fonts
(fonts.gstatic.com) on 2026-10-20. For a distributed/immutable release,
ship the complete upstream OFL.txt for each family alongside these files.
