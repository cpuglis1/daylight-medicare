## Delivered behavior

- The primary invitation is **What has you thinking about Medicare today?** Open text leads; four optional goal shortcuts remain. Clear choices use one Continue and never advance on selection alone.
- A bounded LLM planner interprets explicit multi-fact reports, chooses a permitted ask/explain/prepare move and selects reviewed teaching. Its reason category and every cited concept are validated; arbitrary generated insurance findings and unrestricted tools are absent.
- Related precise values use inline forms. Form submissions and interpreted facts stage a review; edits preserve other work. Unknown/deferred values remain visible. Changing a previously known answer to unknown removes its use by dependent checks while retaining the earlier report for review.
- A relevant work-transition preparation task can be proposed from reported employer coverage; the person explicitly opens it. Existing internal-only questions remain conditional dependencies.
- Preparation is separately reviewed and authorized against the owner and exact input snapshot. It generates an editable, copyable/printable checklist with an actual operation receipt. Changed inputs invalidate a pending authorization, and operation keys prevent duplicate execution. Edited checklist versions remain available within the visit.
- Reviewed education is available without AI or personal intake. Answers stay visible before the next question. Contextual diagrams/timelines, visible sources, Back/edit, draft recovery and optional guide visibility remain available.
- Saving and AI transfer are separate choices. Saving retains the existing seven-day same-browser scope and sensitive-field omission policy. Earlier sensitive values and raw conversation are not automatically attached to model context. No transcript persistence or request-text logging was added.
- The service runs on loopback :8893 with an independent cookie/store/job. The existing aggregate $1/UTC-day cap, pricing/token guards and no automatic AI retries remain. Other Daylight jobs were not restarted.
