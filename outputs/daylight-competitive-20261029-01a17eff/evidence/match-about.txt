		40 heading What we built, Value: 2
			41 text What we built
		42 text Match Your Medicare is a free, neutral place to see which plans fit you before you talk to anyone selling one. You enter your ZIP code, then your doctors, prescriptions and any health conditions you’d like to include.
		43 text Instead of sorting plans by monthly premium, it estimates what each plan would cost you over a whole year (premium, copays and drug costs together), based on what people with similar health conditions and prescriptions actually spend.
