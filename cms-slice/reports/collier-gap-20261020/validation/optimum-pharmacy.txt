Optimum HealthCare, Inc. is an HMO with a Medicare contract and a contract with the state of Florida Medicaid program. Enrollment depends on contract renewal. Optimum HealthCare complies with applicable federal civil rights laws and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability, or sex.
Every year, Medicare evaluates plans based on a 5-star rating system.
Optimum HealthCare, Inc. has been approved by the National Committee for Quality Assurance (NCQA) to operate as a Chronic Condition Special Needs Plan (C-SNP) through 2027 and a Dual Special Needs Plan (D-SNP) through 2027 based on a review Optimum HealthCare, Inc.'s Model of Care.
