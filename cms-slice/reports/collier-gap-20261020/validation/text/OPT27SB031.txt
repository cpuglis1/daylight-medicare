  • Medicare Advantage plans cover everything Original Medicare covers —
     Part A (hospital services) and Part B (medical services) — plus more.

  • Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug Plans cover Medicare Part D drugs
     and Part B drugs.

Don’t miss out on some Extra Help
People with limited incomes may qualify for Extra Help to pay for their
prescription drug costs. If you qualify, Medicare could pay up to 75% or
more of your drug costs including monthly drug plan premiums, yearly
deductibles, and coinsurance. Also, people who qualify won’t have a late
enrollment penalty.
