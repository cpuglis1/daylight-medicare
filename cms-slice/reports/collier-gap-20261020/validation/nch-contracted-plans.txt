Contracted Insurance Plans
Naples Community Hospital, Inc (NCH) and NCH Medical Group are contracted with the following list of health plans. Even though NCH has contracts with these health plans NCH may not be a network provider for ALL products offered by the carrier.
NCH accepts all forms of insurance and we will bill your insurance company accordingly. We also accept Medicare, Medicaid, Champus, Workers Compensation, VA and all other forms of commercial insurance.
It is important to know that not ALL healthcare practitioners who provide services in our hospital participate with the same health insurers, health plans or health maintenance organizations (HMO’s) as the hospital. It is highly recommended that you contact the healthcare practitioners directly to determine which health insurers and health maintenance organizations they participate with as an ‘in-network’ provider.
