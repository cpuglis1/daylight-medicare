Accepted Insurance
Physicians Regional Medical Group accepts most commercial insurance plans, Medicare and self-paying patients. In addition, HMO and PPO plans are accepted based on your insurance carrier. In addition, we provide service for those insured through Community Health Partners.
While we participate with many plans through various networks, we may not participate in all plans provided by an insurance company. Therefore, please contact the member services phone number on your insurance card to determine whether or not we are participants in your plan before scheduling your initial appointment.
For more information, please contact the Physicians Regional Medical Group location where you will be recieving care.
