Eligibility
To get started, see our information below, take our eligibility questionnaire below to have someone contact you, or, please call our main line at 239-985-6400.
$0 Co-pay $0 Deductible $0 Premiums
For those who qualify and live in our counties served.
Who is Eligible?
To be eligible to enroll in Hope PACE you must:
Be at least 55 years old
Be a resident of Charlotte, Collier or Lee county
Be able to live in the community without jeopardizing your health or safety
Have health problems placing you at risk for nursing home care
Program Costs
The program is free of charge for participants with Medicaid coverage. For those with Medicare only or other health insurance, fees will apply. Hope PACE is also open to private pay participants.
If you don’t have Medicaid coverage, please ask us for an estimate of your costs. We can also help explain the Medicaid eligibility requirements in Florida.
Counties Served
We serve Charlotte, Collier, and Lee counties.
