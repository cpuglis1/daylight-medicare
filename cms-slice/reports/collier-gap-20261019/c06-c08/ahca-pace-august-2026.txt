The Florida Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA) serves as the single state agency
responsible for administering Florida’s Medicaid program. Each state must administer their
Medicaid program under a federally approved State Plan. The Medicaid State Plan serves as an
agreement between the state and the federal government describing how that state administers
their Medicaid program. The Program for the All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly (PACE) Program
is included in Florida’s approved Medicaid State Plan.
For a PACE Organization to serve enrollees, the State must establish and maintain a PACE
Program Agreement. A prospective PACE organization submits an application to AHCA for
review. Once the application is judged to be complete, the state issues State Assurance Pages
to the applying organization. At that time, the prospective PACE organization submits their
application to the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS). CMS will review all
documentation and communicate any issues with the organization through a Request for
Additional Information (RAI). The RAI will remain open until such time as CMS determines the
application to be complete and the PACE Program Agreement is issued.
The PACE Program Agreement is the signed three-way agreement between the state
administering agency (AHCA), the PACE organization, and CMS describing how that state
administers their PACE program. The PACE Program Agreement also establishes the group of
individuals covered by each PACE organization and details covered services, payment
methodologies, and other administrative and organizational requirements.
Medicare and Medicaid enrollees are eligible for PACE if they are 55 years of age or older, meet
the nursing facility level of care, reside in a designated service area, and can be safely served in
the community at the time of enrollment. PACE requires the availability of all Medicare and
Medicaid-covered services determined to be medically necessary and covered in the Medicaid
Coverage and Limitations Handbook and fee schedules. PACE organizations are paid a
capitated rate for Medicare services by CMS and a capitated rate for Medicaid services by
AHCA. Because the goal of PACE is to maintain enrollees’ independence outside of a nursing
facility, home and community-based services are provided to participants as needed. The PACE
organization may also deliver other services determined necessary by the interdisciplinary team
to improve and/or maintain the enrollee’s overall health status. Additionally, the PACE enrollee
must accept the PACE center physician as their new primary care physician.
In accordance with proviso following Specific Appropriation 237 of the Fiscal Year 2026-2027
General Appropriations Act (Chapter 2026-232, Laws of Florida), AHCA is submitting this August
2026 Monthly PACE program status report. Specifically, proviso requires AHCA:
               …shall submit a monthly Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly (PACE)
               report detailing all PACE providers. The report shall include each providers
               authorized service area and allocated slots, slots funded, total enrollments,
               actuarial dual rate, estimated monthly claims, and estimated cumulative claims.
               The report shall also include a separate section dedicated to the status of all
               providers with applications pending approval by the agency or the federal
               Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) that includes the name of the
               organization, the service area requested, the number of slots requested, the date
               the agency received the application, the date of agency approval, and the date of
               last submission to the federal CMS. Additionally, the report shall include, by
               county, a listing of PACE providers currently operational or seeking approval in

PACE providers operational or seeking approval, status of operations, number of enrollee per
provider, and the cumulative number of unique individuals served during the fiscal year.
All counties within the state are reflected within the PACE Organization Data by County section

that there are no participants in the identified PACE organization from that particular county.
