## Pre-session evaluation contract

Reuse the existing corpora. Freeze answer keys before the run, then execute the
release-blocking journeys first and the retained regression corpus before final
freeze. Answer keys must be authored independently of the engine result, grounded
in the pinned evidence and reviewed for material Medicare conclusions.

For each fictional profile specify: goal; private ground truth; facts disclosed
over time; exact evidence/method/year; acceptable destinations and plan set or tie;
required qualifications; forbidden conclusions; correction behavior; visible
route and final artifact. The app receives only the opening and subsequent user
answers, never the hidden answer key or preferred plan. A source/method change
requires an explained new key revision, never rewriting expectations to fit a run.

An optimal outcome may be a tie, keep, wait, education-only or a safe hold. A
profile's budget or predictable-cost preference does not establish its chosen
coverage path. Missing critical evidence is a successful safeguard but a blocked
comparison task; report those separately. The engine must not learn to steer every
persona toward one nominated plan.

## Admission and learning

Before sessions, resolve the declared tasks, fictional versus own-situation scope,
consent/data handling, reviewer and release authority. Existing permissioned
history infrastructure is not approval for real-health participant use. A
fictional-case study is an available proposal if real-health gates remain open;
changing to it requires a recorded scope decision, not a quiet fallback.

The intended post-Thanksgiving window crosses recorded review deadlines: retained
PDP/L01 review on November 23 at 7 p.m. Eastern, pinned teaching on November 24
at midnight Eastern, and the hosted model-price review on November 26 at
9:27 a.m. Eastern. Verify each actual binding through the intended session date;
renew through its authorized review or withdraw the affected output. Do not
extend timestamps merely to make a rehearsal or session run.

Proposed reliability bar: no unresolved material factual/privacy/steering failure,
wrong-subject capture, blocked primary action, lost consequential answer or
reproducible loop in the declared core journeys. Label every result complete,
safe-blocked, failed, incomplete or not-run. Cosmetic issues can remain documented.
This bar is a recommendation, not a guarantee of zero bugs or owner acceptance.

Propose 3–5 observed individual sessions in the first wave, then decide whether
to expand toward the existing approximately 20-person cohort. A group introduction
can recruit; personal Medicare conversations need private individual sessions.
Do not coach participants through the path to manufacture completion. Record
every founder intervention and separate assisted from independent success.

The primary question is whether a person can make progress toward an informed
coverage decision through this conversation, with retained control and a useful
next action. Measure baseline and closing teach-back, a deliberate correction,
task completion, trust calibration, abandonment, assistance and staff minutes.
Report first-time, existing-beneficiary and caregiver sessions separately.
Satisfaction and hypothetical willingness to enroll are supporting signals. A
friendly-network pilot cannot establish market demand, comparative superiority,
legal approval or successful end-to-end enrollment. A later independent cohort
and an actual fulfillment study test those claims.

For community discovery, ask for a named organizer, the relevant member/spouse
audience, permission for a later introduction or information session if the pilot
works, and what evidence they need. General enthusiasm is weaker than an agreed
follow-up. Keep relationship access, a conversation, conditional interest, a
committed event, participant use and enrollment distinct in the CRM. No partner
commitments, referrals or messages were created in this review.
