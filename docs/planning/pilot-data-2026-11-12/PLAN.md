November 12, 2026 · D146 · Planning baseline, not implementation or release evidence.

## Outcome and owner direction

Bring useful doctor identification, dated network checks, exact pharmacy selection and reasonable drug estimates into the November 17–December 4 controlled 2026 pilot. Reuse the existing research corpus. Chris selected **directories first, with targeted confirmation**, and **validated average-based drug estimates first** on November 12. ## Geographic and infrastructure design

Start with a declared care-search region: all Collier, all Lee (including Bonita Springs, Estero and Fort Myers), and retained adjacent Hendry, Charlotte, Glades and Monroe evidence. This is a proposed initial search scope, not proof that those counties are complete. Preserve broader Florida/national context and allow an explicit out-of-region doctor search. City names and ZIPs cannot prove county membership. Measure omissions by source and specialty; do not use a short popular-doctor list as the whole market.

Reuse PostgreSQL and the existing Python evidence services. Add normalized name/alias and location indexes with deterministic fuzzy scoring; semantic interpretation can generate candidates but cannot certify identity or participation. No vector service, Dagster/dbt migration or paid vendor is required for the first implementation. Keep plan inclusion independent of match coverage and commercial metadata.

Prepare a versioned additive package containing needed identity/site relationships, L05–L07 lookups, exact controls and evidence locators. Size and benchmark it after inventory; 195 MB is the existing display subset, not the promised size of the expanded package. Retain archival sources locally and give deployed claims resolvable permitted provenance. Public evidence ingestion is separate from permissioned participant facts. Do not put real medication lists, doctors attached to people, transcripts or identifiers into this plan, Brain, sheet, repo or test fixtures.

## Release evidence

Every enabled capability needs a source/version manifest, declared population and missing cases, reproducible fixtures, method review, exact-purpose approval and observed UI/correction behavior. Match confidence, extraction correctness, qualified source listing and consumer acceptance are different states. Record test outcomes and remaining limits, not just counts.
