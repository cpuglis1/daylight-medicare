Each table uses exact offering ID order. **Held** means a charge or term is withheld pending review. The [review record](REVIEW.md) explains the scope and the [evidence packet](display-candidate.json) gives the reason codes and source references for each cell.

The plan premium includes the plan’s Part D premium where applicable. Part B reductions are shown separately. Actual Part B credits depend on member-paid premiums and billing. The medical spending limits cover eligible medical costs and exclude premiums and Part D. A published Part D deductible can depend on tier and exceptions. Drug coverage and fill costs require a separate check.

Primary care, specialist and inpatient figures are for covered in-network care. Emergency and urgent care figures use the selected published visit row. Check plan rules, the applicable setting, referrals, authorization, deductible and any waiver before relying on a charge. The inpatient column describes one acute admission. Transfers, later days and separately billed services can have different terms. Published terms do not quote your bill.
