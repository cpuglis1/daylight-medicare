## What each amount covers

- The plan premium is the monthly charge for the plan itself. Humana uses the published consolidated Part C and Part D premium once. Erickson is a no-Rx plan and uses its published Part C premium. The arithmetic covers January through December 2026 and excludes federal premiums, optional packages, subsidies and adjustments, and medical and drug spending.
- The PCP row covers four ordinary, in-person, covered primary care visits. Humana has a $0 medical deductible. Erickson’s selective deductible excludes these PCP visits. Procedures, lab work, telehealth, specialists and other separately billed services fall outside this component.
- The hospital row counts covered days 1–3 of a single formally ordered acute admission. The discharge day is excluded. For Humana, the $180 daily band covers days 1–5 and the medical deductible is $0. For Erickson, the $1,000 annual medical deductible applies before copayments. If it has yet to be met, the remaining balance and applicable covered charges determine when copays begin. The component excludes professional fees, observation, unrelated services, transfers and readmissions.

The scenario assumes in-network covered services, any required authorization obtained, sufficient room under the annual in-network medical out-of-pocket maximum, and no Medicaid or Qualified Medicare Beneficiary adjustment. Actual eligibility, network participation and authorization still need verification. Erickson has a published Erickson Retirement Community residence requirement. Any applicable state variation and later amendments need separate review.
