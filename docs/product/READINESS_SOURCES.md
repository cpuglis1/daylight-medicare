## Federal vs Florida and current vs future year

The active application has a 2027 Collier sample subset, sourced September 22, 2026, and a pending consumer acceptance registry. The calendar date here is October 2026. A 2026 cost table, the 2026 Florida statute edition and a 2027 plan record are different evidence scopes. No 2026 dollar threshold or income limit is copied into a 2027 estimate. Future-year legal changes and source applicability must be checked before a rule pack is approved. The readiness engine is not the data-acceptance engine.

## S01 · Joining a plan

[Joining a plan](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/get-more-coverage/joining-a-plan) · **Section:** Am I eligible?; Initial Enrollment Period; Other enrollment periods · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

MA requires A+B; standalone drug plans require A or B. Service area and enrollment periods matter. These are distinct from an application or verified eligibility.

## S02 · Plan for Medicare

[Plan for Medicare](https://www.ssa.gov/medicare) · **Section:** Plan for Medicare; When to sign up · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

Age-based and non-age Medicare eligibility exist. An age or a coverage preference alone is not an enrollment determination.

## S03 · When coverage starts

[When coverage starts](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/sign-up/when-does-medicare-coverage-start) · **Section:** Initial Enrollment Period; Signing up for premium-free Part A later; Special Enrollment Period · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

Initial enrollment and start dates depend on circumstances. First-day birthdays and retroactive Part A are material. Do not treat requested coverage month as an effective date.

## S04 · Working past 65

[Working past 65](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/medicare-basics/working-past-65) · **Section:** Health insurance from a job; COBRA; Before you sign up · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

Current-employment group coverage can differ from retiree/individual coverage. Job and coverage end dates can differ. Employer confirmation matters; HSA contributions can be affected by Medicare start dates.

## S05 · Who pays first?

[Who pays first?](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/coordination/who-pays-first) · **Section:** Current/former group coverage; employer-size paths; Federal Program/TRICARE paths · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

The age-65 coordination questions use 20 employees; the under-65 disability path uses 100. Multi-employer and ESRD circumstances need their own analysis. Do not apply one threshold to everyone.

## S06 · COBRA coverage

[COBRA coverage](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/medicare-basics/working-past-65/cobra-coverage) · **Section:** How does COBRA work with Medicare? · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

COBRA is continuation coverage and does not restart the Part B employment-based sign-up period. Possible gaps and payer coordination need review.

## S07 · Retiree insurance

[Retiree insurance](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/medicare-basics/working-past-65/retiree-insurance) · **Section:** Drug coverage; check before making decisions · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

Joining outside coverage can affect retiree/family benefits; obtain benefits-administrator confirmation. Do not infer creditable drug status merely from the word retiree.

## S08 · Creditable drug coverage

[Creditable drug coverage](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/part-d/basics/creditable-coverage) · **Section:** Why do I need creditable coverage?; What you should do · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

A creditable-coverage notice is relevant evidence. A gap of 63 days or more may create a Part D penalty; this is not the same clock as a Part B enrollment period.

## S09 · When can I buy Medigap?

[When can I buy Medigap?](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/medigap/ready-to-buy/when) · **Section:** Federal-rights notice; Advantage trial/history questions; situation-specific answers · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

Trial/guaranteed-issue possibilities depend on the exact event and history. First Medicare Advantage use and prior Medigap matter. Do not generalize one event-specific 63-day result to all cases.

## S10 · Medigap basics

[Medigap basics](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/medigap/basics) · **Section:** Get Medigap Basics; Open Enrollment · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

Federal Medigap open enrollment and later purchase are different. State rights may add protection. Medigap belongs with Original Medicare, not as a new Advantage supplement.

## S11 · Change Medigap policy

[Change Medigap policy](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/medigap/ready-to-buy/change-policies) · **Section:** Can I change my Medigap policy? · **Jurisdiction:** federal · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

Outside protected situations switching can be limited. Do not cancel existing coverage on a provisional match; replacement/free-look details need review.

## S12 · Florida Statutes 627.6741

[Florida Statutes 627.6741](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=&URL=0600-0699/0627/Sections/0627.6741.html) · **Section:** 2026 edition, subsection (1)(a)1 and (1)(a)2; (1)(b) · **Jurisdiction:** Florida · **Retrieved:** 2026-10-22.

## Interpretations explicitly not settled

- Florida §627.6741(1)(a)2 uses a qualifying group-policy termination opportunity measured in months. Federal Medigap situation-specific examples may use days and different plan/history restrictions. Do not pick the longer window, merge them, or calculate a personal last day without a Florida Medicare subject-matter reviewer determining the actual event and applicable law.
- The federal “When can I buy Medigap?” page is an interactive decision tree. A result for one set of answers must not be reused for another. Trial paths, prior policy availability and precise application periods remain expert-review gates. No blanket guaranteed-issue or universal underwriting conclusion.
- Current-employment thresholds and any multi-employer/ESRD payer order require actual employer/program facts. An employee count alone is not sufficient.
- State Medicaid status, QMB/MSP status, Extra Help and SNP alignment are related but distinct. Current monthly/special enrollment rules must be versioned; no legacy quarterly shortcut.
- Drug normalization and provider identification are technical identities, not coverage. Old authorization/exception decisions do not automatically transfer to a new plan.
- No pricing, provider access, eligibility or coverage promise follows merely from completing this check. Official application authorization and final plan confirmation remain separate.
