## Whole-conversation objective · owner direction

On October 25 Chris explicitly directed: design the shortest sufficient conversation; remove duplicate asks, extract multiple facts from one reply, confirm consequential interpretations, skip known or non-applicable fields, explain sensitive or unusual requests briefly, and permit corrections and “I don't know.” Unknown is a valid response and cannot establish readiness. Define which missing facts block which conclusions. Count questions asked, conversation turns and elapsed time, rather than screens. Individual prompt review must satisfy this objective throughout.

### Proposed orchestration rules for the reviewed design

1. Welcome briefly and establish the requested task. Offer useful learning or a direct answer before extensive personal intake when that can be done accurately. Before collecting a personal coverage situation, establish whose situation it is; a helper's knowledge preference does not describe the beneficiary's knowledge or coverage.
2. Evaluate each reply against all relevant existing fields, including later inventory IDs. Reuse explicit, current, confirmed reports for the same person, period and task. Do not repeat a parent or child ask once its required detail is present. One relevant fact may support several checks with its provenance intact.
3. Extract several explicitly volunteered relevant facts from one reply. Distinguish literal reports from interpretation, speculation and externally verified facts. Confirm consequential interpretations, ambiguity or changes before relying on them; a short grouped confirmation can cover related facts. Do not create an extra confirmation turn after every clear inline selection.
4. Ask the next missing detail that can change the requested conclusion or next action. Combine closely related details when a person can answer them naturally, and assess reading, recall and correction burden. Do not put every missing field into a long compound question merely to reduce the number of messages. Preserve atomic field states even when several are collected together.
5. Skip already-known and demonstrably non-applicable details. Not applicable needs a relevant branch reason; silence or an unknown parent fact does not establish it. Optional preferences remain optional. Briefly explain why a sensitive or unusual detail affects the requested check, and request only the minimum needed. Do not infer clinical conditions, age, work status, preferences or eligibility from neighboring reports.
6. Accept corrections and unknown/deferred answers. Re-evaluate only affected dependencies while preserving prior reports. Do not repeatedly ask an unchanged unknown; explain the affected limitation and offer useful permitted work or an optional route to resolve it. A changed task or newly supplied evidence can justify a new question, with a reason.
7. Stop collecting when the requested task's relevant reports suffice, or when a remaining external hold means more intake would not help that task. State what can be concluded, what cannot, and why. Input sufficiency never removes independent source, policy, integration or release holds. A narrower completed learning task must not be counted as completion of a blocked personal recommendation.

**Example, not a scripted universal question:** “I'm new to Medicare and helping my mother review her coverage.” This can supply the current learner's stated familiarity (T02), recipient relationship (RC01) and goal (T01), subject to existing report/confirmation rules. It supplies no specific coverage route, enrollment status, residence or authority to share her data. Do not ask three duplicate inventory questions, and do not infer the unprovided facts.

**Proposed natural opening flow:** T00 welcome with T01 goal in the same opening message → respond to the chosen task with useful context → clarify recipient before personal coverage details → ask only the next consequential missing information. T02 may appear when helpful, but is not automatically the second consumer turn. Subsequent detail requests follow capability dependencies, not the review checklist's order.

### Measurement specification · proposed definitions under the owner-directed metrics

- **Questions asked:** count actual Daylight information requests, including repeated asks and clarifications. Also record displayed prompt count and atomic detail requests: “What ZIP and year?” is one prompt seeking two details. A welcome containing no question adds no information request; packaging many requests into one message cannot hide burden. Record optional asks separately.
- **Conversation turns:** count each submitted user response/inline choice and each assistant message, including explanations, confirmations, corrections and help exchanges. A multi-fact reply is one user turn. Control clicks that form one submitted answer do not become extra turns. Track confirmation/correction turns separately so consolidation can be assessed honestly.
- **Elapsed time:** record wall-clock time from entry into the welcome/requested task to its useful permitted result or explicit blocked/deferred stopping point. Distinguish active interaction, system/external waiting and user pauses; include all in wall-clock totals. Record the reported-input sufficiency milestone separately. Do not report an externally blocked or unknown case as successful completion of the originally requested capability.
- Compare equivalent requested outcomes and case complexity. Report incomplete/deferred cases and correction burden alongside completed ones. No numerical consumer-time target, observed benefit or final instrumentation implementation is established yet. The three-hour design estimate is not a promise that a user session takes three hours.
