# D91 release target · One complete Collier consumer journey

November 4, 2026 · r6

Daylight helps someone understand their coverage options, compare real plans against their needs, and take the next appropriate step for their intended coverage year. The next milestone is to demonstrate that journey with an ordinary Collier first-time enrollee. The [offline 72-offering catalog](../recommendation-scope-2026-11-04-r5/REPORT.md) supplies comparison evidence. The next proof is to turn reported needs into checked facts, explain the resulting tradeoffs, and help the person act on them.

## What the release must do

1. **Set the scope at entry.** Confirm the intended effective date and coverage year before using a plan. Disclose geography, the options represented and material limits. Use 2026 for the existing controlled proof. Route a 2027 shopper to an explicitly year-matched source until an exact 2027 release is supported.
2. **Use the existing profile.** Let home location, coverage situation, doctors, medicines and priorities determine which checks run. Ask a missing question only when its answer could affect the decision or action. Reuse the editable end summary and confirmation. Preserve the existing 41 conditional meanings.
3. **Compare paths, then policies.** Include relevant Original Medicare and MA paths and the option to keep coverage or defer where applicable. Compare actual policies within the supported scope. Start with plan premium, medical deductible and spending limits, Part D coverage, network restrictions, common-care charges and the existing bounded examples. Explain which differences matter given the person's priorities. Keep Part B reductions separate from plan premiums, medical limits separate from drug spending, and scenario components separate from total annual spending.
4. **Resolve consequential personal checks.** Use retained lookups first, then a targeted applicable official source or permissioned founder-assisted verification. Distinguish a confirmed report, an exact source match, a dated listing and a verified conclusion. If an unresolved check could reverse the choice, carry it into a named next action and hold the affected conclusion.
5. **Review what people will see.** Independently review the displayed facts, exact source and method permissions, reusable calculation methods, material exceptions and realistic AI explanations. Exercise corrections, unavailable sources, wrong years and honest unknowns. Release authority must bind the exact evidence, purpose, scope and method, with freshness, expiry and revocation.
6. **Make the path usable through the next action.** Test entry, comparison, source inspection, correction, supported save/return and a concrete action on mobile and desktop. Use an isolated, restorable hosting target with tested privacy and operating limits. Integrated enrollment can follow.

Historical L03 research remains open with its existing r9 denominator and accepted r4 reference. Exhaustive clause and supplemental reconciliation, universal numeric coverage and predicted annual spending are outside the release critical path. Pull a deferred benefit into a case when it could change that person's decision. Keep source stops, explicit acceptance, privacy, independence and protected variants in force.

## Next implementation slice

1. **Start with one decision and two exact plans.** Reuse the r4 pair and existing profile. Set a fixed 2026 scope and a wrong-year case, and choose a supported or targeted path for the doctor, medicine and enrollment questions. Define which personal facts can change the outcome before adding any question. Keep the scope and reasons visible if the relevant market is broader than the compared set.
2. **Complete one manual case before automating more joins.** Use the exact record identifiers above to prepare the verification questions. For the doctor, ask the carrier and office about the named plan, office and effective date. For the drug, check product, quantity, supply and outlet through the year-matched official tool or carrier/PBM. For enrollment, route the specific uncertain date or coverage interaction to the appropriate plan, Medicare or SHIP. If founder assistance is offered, obtain permission first, give the case an owner and response target, record only the necessary facts and preserve the dated answer and its scope. A completed assisted check should return the result to the person and update the comparison. [Medicare Plan Finder](https://www.medicare.gov/plan-compare/) · [Medicare joining and counseling guidance](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/get-more-coverage/joining-a-plan)
3. **Review and wire only the resulting capabilities.** Submit the selected facts, exact checks and methods for independent review. Load their exact purpose authority before real output. Return each check's finding, scope, evidence date, source and next action through the existing backend. Re-evaluate on report correction, newer evidence or revocation. Choose the comparison surface when UI work is separately authorized. See [P06–P12](../../project/COLLIER_MVP.md#actionable-backlog).
4. **Run one complete journey and one material hold/correction case.** In the completed case, the participant can explain the year and relevant coverage paths, why a tradeoff matters, what was checked, and their concrete next step. In the second case, a changed doctor site or medicine form invalidates the old conclusion, and a still-unresolved must-have produces a tracked action instead of a confident recommendation. Also check that a 2027 request cannot see 2026 decision facts as applicable. Observe mobile and desktop use, supported return, and the hosted permission and failure behavior. Record cases attempted and completed, participant comprehension, unsupported claims, effort, staff minutes and unresolved items.
