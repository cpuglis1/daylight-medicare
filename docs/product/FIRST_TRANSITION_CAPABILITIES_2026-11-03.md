## Shared composition and rendering

Composition receives only the current evidence-supported guide titles and blocks, alongside its existing reviewed evidence. Version hashes, maintenance metadata and duplicate source lists stay outside model context. The returned guide uses the same block definitions. The existing validated education update selects the visual; a citation, prior learning exposure or user report alone cannot select it.

New `transition` and `costs` intents join the retained visual vocabulary. Existing education guards continue to prevent unrelated diagrams and repeated unrequested lessons. A failed generation produces no new guide. An old-turn replay rechecks source integrity and suppresses a guide if the conversation has moved on. Rendering uses semantic DOM text and supports the accepted phone/tablet/desktop layout.

Timing reads only current `reportedRows` with `reported` status. It preserves month-only and ordinal text, distinguishes preferred coverage start from actual enrollment, and retains job end separately from insurance end. Unknown, deferred, stale, conflicting and derived rows cannot become calendar events. Open checks are preparation tasks; displaying or reading them records no completed external action.

Guide results are top-level response data. They do not enter `guide`/`record`, saved guest profiles, the comparison evidence source, rankings or recommendation authority. The controller's existing owner/continuation/CSRF/deletion boundaries apply. No new user values are sent to the model by this feature.

## Cost reference and scenario boundaries

`understanding_reference.PublishedCostReference` executes the original unmodified L01 reference module in an isolated interpreter on every lookup. It accepts only year 2026 and optional entry IDs. The original verifier rechecks the exact evidence/code/review, expiry and revocation; the adapter preserves records, qualifications, observations, source conflicts and sources. No income, service dates, research override, personal applicability or total calculation is accepted. This increment supplies a callable backend/CLI adapter, not a new consumer amount endpoint or release of personalized pricing.

`understanding_scenarios.py` uses the existing `comparison_methods.evaluate_cost_scenario`. Demonstration identifiers are explicitly fictional. The examples declare twelve invented $100 monthly payments and either two or twelve invented $25 visits, yielding listed subtotals of $1,250 or $1,500 for 2026. Drug costs and other services remain unknown/excluded. Complete total and quote remain null; no cap, deductible rule, utilization prediction or automatic annualization is applied. Selecting another example is local display state and records no user preference or report.

## Evidence still required

The catalog names **23 required case families** for the eventual capabilities. These are a test/review inventory, not a claim that all personal rules are implemented. The implemented tests cover statement/source integrity, review expiry, supported depth, partial/unknown dates, correction/replay, explicit visual intent, HTTP isolation/saving and fictional arithmetic. Their results do not resolve every family’s Medicare applicability.

Timing still requires L13 review and exact personal rule authority, including its documented source ambiguity and case-specific evidence. Coverage paths need applicable buying/enrollment rights and exact product/access evidence for personal conclusions. Personal costs still need complete accepted benefit meanings, rate/price/assistance inputs and reviewed accumulation/cap methods. Existing L03 work continues at its full frozen scope.

Consumer proof remains separate: observe whether people can identify their next action, explain the relevant coverage choices and distinguish fixed costs from uncertain exposure. Record consequential mistakes, correction success and effort. No comprehension, confidence, savings or recommendation-readiness claim follows from a viewed guide or passing technical tests.
