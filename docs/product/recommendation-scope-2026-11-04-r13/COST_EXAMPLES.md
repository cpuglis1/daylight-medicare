- **Premiums.** Twelve months at the published monthly plan premium. The plan’s Part D premium is included once where applicable. Part B premiums, Part B reductions, optional packages and personal adjustments are separate.
- **Primary care.** Four ordinary covered in-person office visits with in-network primary care. The example covers visit copays only. Labs, procedures and other charges are separate.
- **Inpatient hospital.** One covered in-network acute admission, counted from day one and excluding discharge day. The example assumes any required referral or authorization, enough room under the applicable medical limit, and no assistance adjustment. Facility copays exclude professional fees, transfers, readmissions and later days. A daily copay counts once per covered day. A per-admission copay counts once.

Each column shows a separate component under these assumptions. **Held** means an unresolved input or condition prevents showing an amount. The [structured packet](display-candidate.json) records the reason for each hold.
