## Primary narrative

> We’re building a self-service AI Medicare advisor that helps first-time enrollees understand Medicare and choose coverage through conversation. It meets people where they are, teaches what matters for their situation, and does the research so they can make an informed choice at their own pace.

This is the owner's description of the company being built. It is not a claim that today's prototype has released personal recommendations or enrollment. The approved brand line remains **Your coverage, plain as day**. This organization does not change application copy or design.

## What holds the company together

The first audience is independent people facing their first Medicare transition, particularly where work or family circumstances make the choice consequential. Permissioned help from an adult child or other caregiver remains useful. This focus does not remove existing supported audiences.

The service should reduce the work of understanding and researching Medicare. Conversation is a means of recognizing circumstances, teaching relevant concepts, investigating supported questions and revising a decision. Better understanding and less effort can be valuable even when the final plan is unchanged.

Coverage paths come before policies. Original Medicare, optional Medigap and separate Part D, and Medicare Advantage need fair treatment. Keeping current coverage or deferring while a question is resolved can be a good result. Missing evidence remains unknown rather than becoming a penalty against an option.

The intended business is self-service first, with qualified humans handling supported transactions and exceptions. Commercial relationships belong downstream of the independent decision. Neither commissions nor partner preferences may influence research selection, eligibility, inclusion, ranking, reasons, teaching or substantive advice. The viability of that model across neutral outcomes remains to be demonstrated.

## Company map and data map are different

The owner's **CAP-01–CAP-15 company components** span education, customer understanding, data, advice, service, operations and economics. The established **L01–L15 data layers** describe source domains such as plans, benefits, drugs, providers and rules. The CAP labels organize this assessment only; they do not replace the older C/H/O gap identifiers or create new requirement IDs.

One company component can depend on many data layers. An imported L08 provider dataset, for example, supports acquisition and normalization but does not by itself implement verified doctor coverage, a recommendation, a human handoff or an ongoing customer relationship.

## Claims that still need proof

The repository supports a substantial local advisor and evidence platform. It does not yet substantiate released personal plan recommendations, full-market verified coverage, personal total costs, completed enrollment, an operating human-service organization, improved consumer comprehension, acquisition traction or sustainable unit economics. Use the capability ledger for specific evidence and remaining work. Avoid publishing percentages complete or converting source/test counts into consumer-value claims.
