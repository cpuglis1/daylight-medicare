# Daylight's progressive guide

Current owner direction: D25, D29–D32 in `DAYLIGHT_REQUIREMENTS.md`. This refines the existing warm visual world; it does not replace the journey or introduce decorative gamification.

The guided workspace is **Operate** mode with **Read** teaching content. Arrival remains the existing service introduction. Eight chapters contain several questions where needed; chapter labels describe that structure rather than pretending each chapter is a single question.

## Learning sequence

| Moment | New idea | How the question connects |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First lesson | Medicare is federal health insurance that helps pay for care. | Establish one understandable starting point before personal intake. |
| Recipient, goal, location | Keep the same core picture. | Identify whose situation matters, their goal, and where local plans need checking. |
| Medicare status | Part A is hospital insurance; Part B is medical insurance. | The A/B lines on the card identify what is already in place. |
| Enrollment context | Keep A/B visible; explain why job insurance and dates matter. | Existing insurance and actual dates affect which enrollment questions need confirmation. |
| Medicine needs | Introduce Part D. | Make a list of medicines to check, without implying verified coverage or prices. |
| Other benefits | Add routine dental, vision, and hearing needs. | Keep these separate from A/B and record checking tasks. |
| Priorities | Distinguish monthly premiums and costs when getting care. | Record what matters before ordering published plan facts. |
| Coverage routes | Connect the known pieces to Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage. | Explain the arrangement before asking for a preference. |
| Medigap | Add optional extra insurance for certain Original Medicare costs. | Explain the component and its separate enrollment considerations. |
| Comparison and next steps | Keep the accumulated picture. | Compare published facts and preserve explicit unanswered or unverified checks. |

An already-enrolled person learns the two routes and Medigap before the question about their current arrangement, because those terms are needed to answer it. Subsequent lessons retain those concepts and avoid repeating the introduction unnecessarily.

The experienced comparison shortcut skips beginner lessons. It still checks recipient/location and preserves the factual limitations on eligibility and personal suitability. Viewing a lesson is independent of answering a personal question; it never selects an answer.

## Interaction and finish

- Ask Daylight follows Continue and Back in the task column. There is one corresponding arrival entry below its actions. No duplicate header/lesson-heading help button.
- Every question states its relevance near the heading. The cumulative picture is beside the task on desktop/tablet. Phones show a concise current concept near the question and the full accumulated picture after the task.
- Learned concepts survive Back independently of personal answers; Start over clears both learning complexity and session answers.
- Preserve the existing Fraunces headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible interface text, warm paper, sage, olive, and sun identity. The service finish uses a consistent header rule, compact chapter navigation, aligned forms, clearer type hierarchy, restrained outlines, and an olive primary action.
- Service credibility comes from clear behavior, sources, accessible controls, and honest limits. No invented accreditation, approval, testimonials, enrollment ability, or live availability.
- Current service values are in the D25/D29–D32 section of `cms-slice/apps/chat/styles.css`. No framework migration, new dependency, raster imagery, or decorative animation is needed for this refinement.

The visual teaching describes Medicare generally. Entered facts are not independent verification, and a lesson never establishes personal eligibility, coverage, dates, or costs.
