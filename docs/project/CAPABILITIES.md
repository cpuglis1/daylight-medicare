# Progress against the fifteen company capabilities

November 2, 2026. Assessment of the local Daylight workspace against the owner's company map. **Progress is substantial but uneven: the advisor experience and research foundation are much further along than accepted personal decisions, operating service and business proof.** The task now is to connect and validate those parts, with clear owners, rather than add another broad product specification.

The [primary narrative](../company/README.md) describes the intended company. The table distinguishes current mechanics from that completed promise. “Working locally” means implemented behavior with inspected code or scoped technical evidence; it does not mean production, independent domain acceptance or demonstrated consumer benefit. “Partial foundation” means important pieces exist but the end-to-end task is incomplete. “Specified” means strategy/contracts dominate the available evidence. No overall percentage is justified across these unlike capabilities.

## What is strong and what is slowing progress

The project has invested in the difficult foundations: preserving conditions rather than flattening benefits, keeping unknown evidence visible, retaining corrections, isolating commercial incentives and restricting what can become a personal finding. The full local database and internal research bridge are real advances. The advisor is substantially more capable than the older README's “visual guide with help” description.

The largest product gap is the distance between **describing circumstances conversationally** and **finishing accepted personal research with an understandable choice**. The existing engine intentionally keeps the six consumer purposes held: display, comparison, ranking, recommendation, quote and enrollment. Separately gated L01/r4 reference readers have their own exact acceptance; those receipts do not release the current research bridge or its projections. Unblocking the complete flow takes reviewed semantics, identity/temporal joins, decision methods and release machinery, not only another interface improvement.

The largest organizational gap is reproducibility. A collaborator cannot recover the current advisor, evidence selection and permissions from the small committed repository alone. Current navigation mixes dates and variants, local paths are integral to some evidence bindings, and a decoding backup has not been restored on a clean machine. These are tractable engineering tasks and can progress while substantive source review continues.

The largest business uncertainty is consumer and operating proof. There is no demonstrated cohort in the reviewed material showing that people understand more, make fewer consequential errors, finish useful work with manageable effort, or can be served sustainably. The company thesis is coherent; its benefit and economics need observation.

## Data coverage is not one completion badge

The [detailed L01–L15 review](../reviews/2026-11-02-project-readiness/DATA_REVIEW.md) records current selected populations and remaining conditions. Important distinctions:

- L01 has an accepted **published federal reference**. That is not a personal entitlement or cost calculator.
- L02 retains **72 observed MA offerings**, with market completeness and verified availability still unresolved; L04 retains **10 PDP identities**. The 72 are neither a verified full consumer market nor the whole coverage-path universe.
- L03 acquired all **144 required main SB/EOC document slots**. Full semantic reconciliation is still open. The r12 pilot and any proposed atomic children cannot silently change r9's **392,570 obligations**.
- L05–L08 support real drug/pharmacy/provider research, but criteria stops, units, exact network/site/period ties and individual access/cost findings remain material gaps.
- L09 source/parity work is further along than its old README suggests. L10 Medigap has substantial filing evidence but an unknown all-market denominator, source-period conditions and no binding quote engine.
- L11–L14 retain useful rule references; personal event/date/application interpretations and exact review remain distinct. L15's dated counseling contacts are neither appointment availability nor an operating Daylight handoff service.

## How progress should be reported from here

Use capability-specific deliverables with an owner, scope/year, exact code/data versions, observed result and remaining limit. Keep five states separate: implemented, technically checked, domain reviewed, accepted for a named use, and proven useful to users. A single “done” checkbox loses the distinctions most important to Daylight.

Use the [Collier pilot backlog](COLLIER_MVP.md) to join the existing workstreams. The new company map is an index across that work, not fifteen new software services, fifteen mandatory intake stages, or permission to reduce the existing data program.
