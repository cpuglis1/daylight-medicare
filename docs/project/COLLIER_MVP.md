Owner confirmed November 2, 2026: **2026 controlled pilot first**. The initial audience is first-time Medicare enrollees researching independently, with permissioned caregiver participation where relevant. The company promise remains useful education and completed research leading to an informed coverage choice at the person's pace.

The current advisor can support a bounded education/preparation evaluation after study/privacy readiness is established. It cannot yet support a claim of an accepted personal-choice MVP. The milestones below distinguish that early learning opportunity from the complete coverage-choice service. ## Scope decisions already made

- Geography: Collier County for the controlled pilot. National teaching remains distinct from county/year-specific plan facts.
- Benefit year: 2026. Retained 2027 sample records stay visibly separate and cannot supply missing 2026 facts. Any later 2027 expansion needs its own exact identity, benefit, formulary, network, pricing and policy work.
- Primary development surface: :8893 advisor-first; protect :8890, :8891, :8896 and their stores. This is not a declaration that :8893 is a public production service.
- Intake: retain natural report capture and targeted ambiguity clarification. The latest owner direction requires one editable end summary and confirmation immediately before requested personal comparison/recommendation; not a new universal questionnaire or per-message acceptance cards.
- Coverage scope: paths before policies, including Original Medicare with optional Medigap/separate Part D, MA and relevant other coverage. Current coverage, keep and defer remain legitimate outcomes.
- Independence: commercial economics do not enter substantive decisions, AI context, selection or research priorities. A preferred fulfillment route cannot narrow the evidence universe.

## Pilot measurement and decision rules

Before recruiting, agree the actual tasks, participant count, observation window, qualified reviewer and decision thresholds. This review does not invent a passing score or claim a cohort occurred. Use the [existing cohort definitions](../product/STRATEGIC_THESIS.md#evidence-definitions-for-the-first-cohort):

| Measure | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Factual correctness | Reviewed claims/tasks, material errors by severity, unsupported statements, appropriate abstention and corrections. |
| Understanding | Participant explanation of coverage paths, meaningful tradeoffs and unresolved checks; confidence separately. |
| Control | Successful fact/priority corrections, permission understanding, recovery without restart and incorrect confirmations. |
| Useful completion | Requested supported tasks completed, failures, abandonment, time/effort and remaining steps. Enrollment alone is not success. |
| Operational effort | Staff minutes, source review/maintenance time, repeated work and exception cause. |
| Economics | Acquisition, AI, hosting, licensed support and maintenance costs across the neutral outcome mix. |
| Continuing value | Later authorized use and resolved changed circumstances; distinguish actual follow-up from intent. |

For interaction research, compare the excellent adaptive form, directed conversation and hybrid using the same evidence and confirmation standards. Test the value of evidence depth separately with interface held fixed. A small operational pilot can establish feasibility; it cannot by itself prove comparative superiority.
