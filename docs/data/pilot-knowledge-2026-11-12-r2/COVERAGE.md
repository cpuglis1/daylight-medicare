# Supplied study-guide coverage and corrections

The user's S3-III excerpt (pages 17–19) is the coverage checklist. Official
sources support the indexed wording; the guide itself is not the authority.
All requested topics below are represented without changing the original 43
records. New facts remain candidates for exact acceptance.

| Material from the guide | Indexed facts | Qualification retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Original Medicare is A and B | `pk_parts`, `pk_routes` | Separate from MA and Medigap. |
| A inpatient, SNF, hospice, some home health | `pk_part_a_benefits`, `pk_snf` | Qualifying inpatient stay; observation does not count; approved waivers; other skilled-care conditions. |
| A premium-free for most with 40 quarters | `pk_part_a_premium` | Qualifying own/family record; work-credit rules differ for disability/ESRD; premium-free is not all care free. |
| B doctors, outpatient, preventive and DME | `pk_part_b_benefits_premium` | Particular service rules still apply. |
| B standard 2026 premium $202.90 | `pk_part_b_benefits_premium` | Year-specific standard, not every person's actual bill. |
| C private A/B, usually D | `pk_routes`, `pk_part_c_premium` | Part B premium obligation plus any plan premium; assistance/reductions can change actual payment. |
| D outpatient drugs, private standalone/MA drug plans | `pk_part_d_optional` | Some drugs are covered through A/B; a particular drug is not verified. |
| D optional, not automatically added by A | `pk_part_d_optional`, `pk_creditable` | Medicaid/Extra Help automatic enrollment is an exception; delaying can incur a penalty. |
| Eligibility at 65 | `pk_iep`, `pk_join`, `pk_part_a_premium` | Age is not the only prerequisite; part and entitlement basis matter. |
| Disability after 24 benefit months; ALS waiver | `pk_disability_eligibility` | Benefit entitlement, not diagnosis/application date; ALS begins with disability-benefit entitlement. |
| ESRD eligibility | `pk_esrd_eligibility` | Separate application, record and treatment requirements; not automatically immediate on diagnosis. |
| No long-term custodial care | `pk_custodial`, `pk_snf` | Distinguish covered short-term skilled care and conditional Medicaid assistance. |
| Initial enrollment seven months | `pk_iep`, `pk_start`, `pk_first_day_birthday`, `pk_initial_plan_window` | Separate A/B and plan enrollment; first-day birthday shifts the window. |
| Initial coverage start | `pk_start`, `pk_initial_plan_window` | The guide's “month after signup” shortcut is not the premium-free Part A rule. |
| General enrollment Jan 1–Mar 31, next month | `pk_gep` | For B and premium-A; possible SEP/penalty exceptions. |
| Current-work SEP and eight-month clock | `pk_work_sep`, `pk_work_sep_start` | Earlier of job/coverage ending; disability large-group condition; IEP/ESRD exceptions; start-date election. |
| COBRA/retiree do not extend work SEP | `pk_cobra`, `pk_work_sep_start` | Creditable drug coverage and plan SEPs are separate. |
| Annual Oct 15–Dec 7, Jan 1 start | `pk_aep`, `pk_enrollment_windows` | Plan window does not itself authorize first B enrollment. A separate personal IEP/SEP can overlap those dates, so “never in these months” would be wrong. |
| MA OEP Jan 1–Mar 31, one change, next month | `pk_ma_oep`, `pk_ma_oep_effective` | Existing MA only; initial three-month opportunity; MSA exception; no automatic Medigap protection. |
| Medigap six months, 65+ and B | `pk_medigap_window`, `pk_medigap_window_scope` | Federal health-based-denial protection; available policies; actual policy start and waiting-period rights separate. Florida holds preserved. |
| A penalty 10%, twice years delayed | `pk_penalty_a` | Only people who must buy A; qualifying exceptions. |
| B 10% per full 12 months | `pk_penalty_b` | Countable periods, not simply years since birthday; SEP/MSP protections; generally while B. |
| B 24 months = 20%; 23 = 10% | `pk_penalty_b` | General arithmetic only, assuming all delay months count and no exemption. |
| D 1% per full uncovered month after 63-day gap | `pk_penalty_d` | National base premium, not plan premium; rounding; Extra Help; monthly duration generally while D. |
| “Forever” penalty mnemonic | `pk_penalty_b`, `pk_penalty_d` | Avoid an unconditional lifetime claim; keep exemptions and duration qualified. |
| Age 65+ current-work 20-employee threshold | `pk_payer_order` | Own/spouse's current work; multi-employer and actual counting rules. |
| Under-65 disability 100-employee threshold | `pk_payer_disability` | Own/family member's current work; multi-employer condition and counting rules. |
| ESRD 30-month coordination | `pk_payer_esrd` | All employer sizes, including COBRA/retiree; clock tied to eligibility; prior-primary exception preserved. |
| Retiree/COBRA generally secondary | `pk_payer_retiree_cobra`, `pk_cobra` | ESRD exception and plan terms; no personal claim determination. |
| Workers' comp/no-fault/liability first | `pk_payer_injury` | Only related illness/injury; conditional payments and recovery, not automatic payment. |
| Formulary and tiers | `pk_formulary`, `pk_formulary_tiers` | Lower tiers generally cost less; plan-specific structures. |
| Prior authorization, step therapy, quantity limits | `pk_drug_rules` | Exact plan/drug/diagnosis required; no clinical recommendation or automatic approval. |
| Formulary exception and restriction waiver | `pk_formulary_exception` | Part D drug, medical support and coverage determination; excluded drugs are not automatically made covered. |
| Tiering exception and prescriber statement | `pk_tiering_exception` | Preferred alternatives less effective/adverse effects; not every tier qualifies. |
| Medigap sold since 2006 excludes drugs | `pk_medigap_drug_exclusion` | Older policies require their own review. |
| Part D annual cap started $2,000 in 2025 | `pk_part_d_cap` | $2,100 for 2026; covered Part D spending, eligible third-party payments and calendar-year scope. Not a premium/all-health-cost cap. |
| Medicaid: funding and administration | `pk_medicaid_governance` | Federal/state funding; state administration within federal rules; not a Florida eligibility ruling. |
| Social Security: strict disability definition | `pk_ssa_disability_definition` | Substantial gainful work, medical impairment, 12 months/death, previous/other work and special rules. |
