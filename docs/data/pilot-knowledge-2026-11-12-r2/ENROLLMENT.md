# Enrollment windows by situation

Six principal windows from the supplied guide, separated into 16 pilot situations. These are general rules and prerequisites, not personal eligibility determinations or a new date calculator. New wording is staged for acceptance; the original 40 facts remain live.

| Situation | Window and allowed action | Coverage starts | Conditions | Indexed evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First eligible at 65: Part B or premium-Part A | Generally three months before the birthday month, that month and three months after. Enroll in Part B and, if needed, premium-Part A. | Before the eligibility month: that month. During or after it within the IEP: next month. | First-day birthday shifts the clock. Automatic enrollment and earlier disability/ALS/ESRD eligibility need separate handling. | `pk_iep`, `pk_start`, `pk_first_day_birthday` |
| First eligible at 65 with a birthday on the first | Four months before the birthday month through two months after it. Use the shifted initial enrollment window. | Generally the first of the month before the birthday month, subject to the part and enrollment timing. | Late Part B is not automatically backdated. Use the existing deterministic date helper for personal dates. | `pk_first_day_birthday`, `pk_start` |
| Eligible for premium-free Part A at 65 | Initial enrollment or any time later once eligible. Enroll in premium-free Part A. | Generally the age-65 eligibility month; later applications can be retroactive up to six months, no earlier than initial eligibility. | First-day birthdays shift eligibility. HSA contributions require separate attention to retroactive coverage. | `pk_start`, `pk_hsa`, `pk_part_a_premium`, `pk_first_day_birthday` |
| First Medicare Advantage or standalone Part D plan | Initial plan-enrollment opportunity around first Medicare eligibility; generally seven months for age-65 eligibility. Join MA with both A and B, or standalone Part D with A or B. | A request before required Medicare starts takes effect with it; a request after generally starts the month after plan receipt. | Plan service area and prerequisites apply. This is separate from signing up for Parts A and B. | `pk_initial_plan_window`, `pk_join` |
| Under 65 with qualifying disability-benefit entitlement | For the ordinary disability route, three months before the 25th benefit month through three months after it. A/B are generally automatic; make separate health/drug-plan choices if applicable. | Generally the 25th month of benefit entitlement, or age 65 if earlier; plan start follows its own request timing. | Not 24 months after diagnosis or application. ALS and ESRD differ. | `pk_disability_eligibility`, `pk_initial_plan_window` |
| ALS with disability-benefit entitlement | The ordinary 24-month Medicare waiting period is waived; confirm the entitlement notice and plan opportunity. Receive Medicare through the ALS disability pathway; plan selection remains separate. | First month of disability-benefit entitlement. | Diagnosis alone does not establish an award or effective date. | `pk_disability_eligibility` |
| Medicare based on ESRD | Separate ESRD eligibility and application rules; do not substitute the age-65 IEP or work SEP. Apply based on qualifying treatment and work/benefit record. | Usually the fourth dialysis month; qualifying home training or transplant circumstances can allow an earlier start. | No personal date calculated. Payer coordination is a separate 30-month rule with prior-primary exceptions. | `pk_esrd_eligibility`, `pk_payer_esrd` |
| Delayed Part B while covered through current employment | After the IEP: while qualifying group coverage continues, or eight months after employment or coverage ends, whichever is first. Enroll in Part B under a qualifying work SEP without a late penalty. | Generally next month; while still working or within the first full month after coverage ends, a start delayed up to three months may be requested. | COBRA/retiree coverage does not extend it. Disability family-member coverage needs large-group status. Not the ESRD rule or a Part D deadline. | `pk_work_sep_start`, `pk_work_sep`, `pk_cobra` |
| Missed initial Part B or premium-Part A enrollment | General Enrollment Period: January 1–March 31 each year, unless a qualifying SEP provides another route. Enroll in Part B and/or premium-Part A. | Month after sign-up. | A late penalty may apply; do not assume no SEP or exemption without the coverage history. | `pk_gep`, `pk_penalty_a`, `pk_penalty_b` |
| Annual Medicare health/drug-plan selection or changes | October 15–December 7. Join, switch or drop eligible MA/Part D coverage, including changing between Original Medicare and MA. | January 1 of the following year if the plan receives the request by December 7. | This window does not itself permit first Part B enrollment or guarantee Medigap. A person's IEP or SEP can independently overlap these dates. | `pk_aep`, `pk_join` |
| Already in Medicare Advantage | January 1–March 31 MA Open Enrollment. One change to another MA plan or to Original Medicare, with an opportunity to join separate Part D. | First of the month after the plan receives the request. | Not a route from Original Medicare into MA or a general standalone Part D switch window. Medigap and Medical Savings Account plan rules are separate. | `pk_ma_oep_effective`, `pk_ma_oep`, `pk_medigap_underwriting` |
| Joined MA when first eligible for Medicare | First three months with Parts A and B for the new-beneficiary MA open-enrollment opportunity. Change MA plans or return to Original Medicare, with a separate drug-plan opportunity. | Generally first of the month after plan receipt. | Separate trial rights may also apply; this row does not adjudicate Medigap rights. | `pk_ma_oep_effective` |
| Already has Part A and adds Part B later | An initial MA opportunity tied to first having both parts; exact closing-date wording needs reconciliation before a personal deadline is given. Join MA during the applicable initial plan window. | With Part B for a request before B starts; generally next month for a request after B starts. | The official webpage and booklet describe the endpoint differently. No exact duration is indexed as accepted wording. This does not reset Part D eligibility or create a Part B SEP. | `pk_new_part_b_plan_window`, `pk_join` |
| Leaving employer/union coverage, including COBRA | Plan SEP continues for two full months after the month coverage ends. Join an eligible MA or drug plan. | Generally first of the month after plan receipt. | This is not the work-based Part B SEP. Avoid a 63-day gap in creditable drug coverage; the plan SEP does not automatically waive penalties. | `pk_plan_coverage_loss_window`, `pk_creditable`, `pk_cobra` |
| Involuntary loss of creditable drug coverage or notice it is no longer creditable | Two full months after the month of loss or notification, whichever is later. Join MA with drug coverage or a standalone drug plan, subject to prerequisites. | Generally first of the month after plan receipt. | Keep the notice and actual end date. The 63-day penalty trigger is not the same as this enrollment deadline. | `pk_plan_coverage_loss_window`, `pk_penalty_d` |
| At least 65 and newly enrolled in Part B | Six months beginning with the first month both conditions are met. Buy an available Medigap policy under the federal open-enrollment protection against health-based denial. | Confirm the issued policy's effective date; the buying window itself is not an effective-date promise. | Not annual. Starting B while working can start the clock. Other buying rights and pre-existing-condition questions remain separate; all three Florida holds stay disabled. | `pk_medigap_window_scope`, `pk_medigap_window`, `pk_medigap_underwriting` |

The full source passages and dates are in [catalog.json](catalog.json). The situation map is [machine readable](enrollment-situations.json). Its fact references preserve the same evidence/acceptance boundary; it is not separately injected into live model context.

The first-Part-B-to-MA closing-date detail remains deliberately unspecified: the official webpage and booklet describe the endpoint differently. The covered benefit prerequisites and start-date branches agree. This is an extra branch beyond the six windows in the supplied guide; do not use the differing wording to calculate a personal last day.
