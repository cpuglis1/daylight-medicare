# Review protocol for the working research slice

Prepared October 17, 2026. This is a proposed review protocol using real delivered evidence. No participants, completion rates, approvals or consumer outcomes have been recorded. It does not authorize a public pilot or replace qualified domain/accessibility review.

## Entry conditions

Use the source-pinned local build and its manifests. Confirm the current verification commands pass, record their hashes and use public examples only. A reviewer may inspect the current limits without supplying a real person's prescriptions, diagnoses, Medicare number or authority documents. Testing real saved/private cases waits for the identity, consent, retention and access controls in package 6.

Include direct retirees researching for themselves and caregivers researching with or for someone else. Record those journeys separately. Caregiver acquisition priority does not remove retiree participation, imply that relatives have legal authority or require a caregiver for the direct retiree flow. Recruitment and study consent require an actual accountable study owner; none has been appointed here.

## Concrete tasks and evidence

| Task in the current local view | Evidence the reviewer should be able to find | Misunderstanding that requires correction |
|---|---|---|
| Explain what the Naples list covers | 2027; Collier FIPS 12021; 70 source records, 38 comparison records and 32 visible exclusions | Treating excluded categories as personal ineligibility, full Medicare market coverage or a suitability ranking |
| Compare H5434-002, H5434-042 and S5904-001 | MA-PD, MA-only and PDP are different structures; premiums use different source fields; inapplicable fields remain inapplicable | Treating a PDP's missing medical spending limit as zero or treating these three records as complete interchangeable coverage bundles |
| Inspect a monetary fact and its source | Exact original CSV column/ordinal/archive hash; only specific fields on five records have carrier corroboration | Assuming source identity, a successful hash check or the carrier sample certifies all benefits, all records or personal fit |
| Choose among the public Trulicity examples | Four actual strength/form concepts; none confirmed for a person; exact plan/product lookup | Treating a brand name as a complete prescribed medicine or accepting the default example as a recommendation |
| Explain the inspected quantity restriction | CMS raw amount 2 over 28 days; carrier 4 pens over 28 days; unit unresolved | Choosing a conversion without evidence or claiming a prescription's quantity is accepted |
| Explain the pharmacy result | Exact public pharmacy NPI/location; matching dated plan-network row; current store operation unconfirmed | Treating NPI identity as proof of clinical provider participation, licensure or a current appointment |
| Locate the price limitation | No captured 2027 drug-price source and no personal annual cost | Using a cost-share schedule as an actual fill quote, treating unavailable as free or applying the 2026 quarterly source to 2027 |
| Describe the eventual purchase path | Ready to purchase requires a qualified licensed, appointed agent-of-record session, full context, review/re-verification and explicit authorizations | Assuming checkbox selection, AI prose, caregiver relationship or a source-status token authorizes submission |

Observe source retrieval, mobile/keyboard use, comprehension and the person's stated uncertainty separately. Record the task, exact artifact/version, actual response, correction needed and reviewer disposition. A task result is not inferred from a click, a favorable comment or the engineer's own ability to navigate the page. The existing Chrome screenshots are engineering checks, not participant evidence.

## Stop and repair conditions

Stop the affected capability when a review finds the wrong year/plan/location, missing data presented as zero/coverage, a misleading premium basis, unsupported quantity conversion, an inaccessible critical source/exclusion, or a purchase action presented as fulfillable without operating support. Preserve the case and source versions, correct the cause and rerun the affected evidence. Qualified owners must set and approve launch thresholds; this document does not invent passing rates or claim sign-off.

When AI is implemented, add held-out tests that compare every structured and prose option list with the exact engine snapshot. A change in compensation/partner context must leave membership/order unchanged. Criteria/weight suggestions remain unconfirmed until the person confirms them; a changed basis creates a new snapshot. The current source-order view does not satisfy those future ranking or model evaluations.

## Later operating and economic acceptance

Before a live purchase test, provide actual state/product/channel authority, current agent qualifications/appointments, carrier access, staffed capacity and an approved consent/review/submission process. Exercise real timeout/duplicate/uncertain submission reconciliation in an authorized environment. A draft protocol, simulated receipt or local button cannot establish these capabilities.

Declare the acquisition/handoff cohort, observation window, policy/component counting, shared-labor allocation and source of effective/retained status before calculating the primary metric. Include attributable review, re-verification, failed/abandoned cases, follow-up, corrections, service and exception minutes. Keep queue waiting separate. With no effective retained policies, report the ratio as undefined and show actual work/case counts; do not report zero minutes per retained policy.

Review comprehension, suitability/corrections, complaints, retention, response time, abandonment and fully allocated contribution alongside labor. Use actual carrier/status and finance evidence. The 12–24 month incumbent-response window remains a dated hypothesis. Financial-advisor/publisher distribution follows evidence from direct use; health-system expansion needs actual outcome evidence and its own role/data agreements.
